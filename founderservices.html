Where AI-built apps usually fail

None of these show up in a demo. All of them show up once real people use the app.

Customers can see each other's data

A user changes one number in the web address and sees someone else's orders, messages or invoices.

What it costs you: customer trust, and possibly legal trouble if personal data leaks.

Your business lives inside one platform

Hosting, database and the tool you fix bugs with all sit in one account you don't control. On Lovable, your live app and your next fix are paid from the same credits. On Bubble, you never get the code.

What it costs you: prices, limits and rules can change, and you can't simply pick up and leave.

Your secret keys are visible

The keys to your database, payments or email service sit in code that anyone curious enough can read.

What it costs you: someone else can run up your bills or read your data.

There's no real way back

A bad change, by you or by the AI, deletes or scrambles data. Nobody has ever tested restoring it.

What it costs you: you find out the day you need it.

It breaks when people actually show up

Fine with ten test users, slow or failing at five hundred. Emails don't arrive. Payments go through, but the order never appears.

What it costs you: lost sign-ups on the day that matters most.

Every fix breaks something else

The app has grown past what the AI tool can keep track of. You spend more on repairs than on new features.

What it costs you: progress stalls, and so does your runway.

Questions founders ask

I'm not technical. Will I understand what you find?

Yes. Every problem is explained in plain words: what it is, what could happen to your business, and what it costs to fix. Technical notes are added for whoever does the fixing.

What happens to my app if I stop paying the platform?

It depends on the platform. On Lovable, your published site stays online and your code and data are kept, but some features stop on the free plan, and hosting and the built-in backend draw on the same credits you build with. On Bubble, moving to the free plan switches off your custom domain and blocks data export until you upgrade again. Either way, you depend on rules you don't set. The moving plan shows exactly where you stand.

Do I have to leave Lovable, Bubble or the tool I used?

No. Many apps are fine where they are once the gaps are fixed. If moving isn't worth it for you, I'll say so.

Will my app go offline while you work on it?

No. I work on a copy first and switch over only when it's tested and you've approved it.

Who owns the code and the data?

You do, always. I work in your accounts with access you can remove at any time. An NDA is available before you share anything.
