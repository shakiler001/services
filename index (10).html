How an engagement runs

The same four steps apply whether it’s a $900 review or a monthly retainer.

  1. Brief

    You describe the system and the problem in a message or a 30-minute call. No charge.

  2. Proposal

    Within two working days you get scope, price and timeline in writing.

  3. Work

    Regular written updates and a weekly call. You see progress before you see an invoice.

  4. Handover

    Deliverables, documentation and a walkthrough, so your team can carry on without me.

Payment

Fixed-price work is paid 50% to start and 50% on delivery. Packages over $2,000 can be split into milestones. Monthly plans are invoiced at the start of each month, and hourly work weekly.
